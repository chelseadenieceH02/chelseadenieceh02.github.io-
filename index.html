Our Story

A Small Bakery With a Big Oven

Benji Bakery is a local, family-run bakery making fresh bread, pastries, and custom cakes every day. What started as weekend baking for friends has grown into a neighbourhood favourite, and now we're bringing that same care online so it is easier than ever to see what's fresh and place an order.
