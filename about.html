Our Story

About Benji Bakery

Fresh bread, honest ingredients, and a genuine love for baking - that's what Benji Bakery has been about since day one.

How We Started

Benji Bakery began as weekend baking for family and friends before growing into a full neighbourhood bakery. Word of mouth about our sourdough and custom cakes spread quickly, and soon our little kitchen wasn't big enough to keep up with the orders coming in over the phone and social media.

Today, we bake fresh bread, pastries, and made-to-order cakes every day, using the same recipes and the same attention to detail we started with. This website exists so it is easier than ever for customers to see what's fresh, learn our story, and place custom cake orders without having to call or message us directly.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To bake fresh, honest food every day and make it simple for our neighbours to find, order, and enjoy it - whether that's a loaf of bread for tonight's dinner or a cake for a once-a-year celebration.

Vision

To be the bakery our community thinks of first: known for consistent quality, friendly service, and a menu that's clear and easy to trust, including clear allergen information for every item.
